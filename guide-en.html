Why nobody sees your posts

7 reasons social media isn't bringing clients to your small business — and what to fix today.

1

Your first line doesn't stop the scroll

People decide in 2 seconds. "Hello dear followers" means keep scrolling. A pain point or a sharp question means stop.

Fix today: start your next post with the question your client asks herself at 11 PM.
2

Your post doesn't say what to do next

A beautiful post gets seen — then what? If it doesn't say "book now", "DM me", or "save this", nobody will.

Fix today: one simple action at the end of every post. One, not three.
3

You post "whenever there's time"

Two silent weeks and your reach drops — accounts that go quiet lose visibility within 2–3 weeks. Consistency beats genius.

Fix today: 3 posts a week, every week — better than 7 this week and zero the next.
4

Content is about you, not your client

"We opened in 2019" is interesting to you. Your client cares about "broken nail two days before the wedding — what now?"

Fix today: take the 3 questions clients ask you most often — that's 3 ready-made posts.
5

Photos only — the weakest format

Single photos get far less reach than short videos and carousels. The algorithm promotes what holds attention.

Fix today: turn the same photos into a carousel that tells a story — instantly more engagement.
6

You post at random times

The same post at 3 PM Tuesday and at 7:30 PM gets different reach. Your audience scrolls in the evening and at lunch.

Fix today: videos in the evening 7–9 PM, helpful guides at lunchtime.
7

Your bio doesn't turn visitors into clients

Someone lands on your profile from a video — and in 3 seconds can't tell who you are, how you help, or where to tap. No follow.

Fix today: first line of bio = who you help and with what. Last line = one link with a clear action.
